Tours and activities in Sri Lanka

Ceylon Nooks is a marketplace for tours and activities in Sri Lanka, run with local guides, drivers and hosts. Book a sunrise climb of Sigiriya Rock, whale watching in Mirissa, the train from Kandy to Ella or a cooking class in Galle, and pay one clear price with taxes and fees included.

Read more

Sri Lanka packs ancient cities, tea country, rainforest, wildlife parks and long beaches into a small island, so most trips combine several regions. The cultural triangle (Sigiriya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa and Anuradhapura) is best from January to April. The hill country around Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Ella is cooler all year. The south and west coasts are driest from December to March, and the east coast from May to September.

Every host on Ceylon Nooks can chat with you before you book. Many show their Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority registration and police clearance on their profile, and many experiences offer free cancellation or book now, pay later.

Ways to save

  • Travel from May to September for the east coast and north. Prices drop on the west and south coasts too.
  • Share a driver with friends. A car with a driver costs the same for one person or four.
  • Take the train for the long, scenic stretches. Even first class is under USD 15.
  • Carry some cash. Small guesthouses, tuk-tuks and markets rarely take cards.
  • Book tours with free cancellation, so you’re not out of pocket if your plans change.

Before you book

Something else on your mind? Visit the help centre or message us.

What does “local hosts only” mean?

Every tour and activity on Ceylon Nooks is run by a Sri Lankan person, family or business. Their name is on the listing, and many show their Sri Lanka Tourism registration and police clearance on their profile.

Is the price I see the price I pay?

Yes. The price includes our service fee and any VAT. Nothing is added at checkout.

Can I talk to my host before booking?

Yes. Message them from the listing about pickups, food, kids or anything else, before you pay.

What if my plans change?

Most experiences have free cancellation up to 48 hours before the start, less a 5% payment processing fee. Some hosts have their own policy, shown on the listing.

Do I have to pay everything upfront?

Not always. Where a host offers Reserve now, pay later, your card is charged automatically 5 days before your experience.

Sri Lanka holidays, planned with local people

Sri Lanka packs ancient cities, tea-covered hills, wildlife parks and two coastlines into an island you can cross in a day. Ceylon Nooks brings together local tours and activities, all run by Sri Lankan hosts you can message before you book.

Most first trips loop through the Cultural Triangle, the Hill Country and the south coast in ten to fourteen days.

Read more about planning a Sri Lanka holiday

The Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa and Anuradhapura) is best from January to April. The Hill Country around Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Ella is cooler all year. The south and west coasts are driest from December to March, and the east coast from May to September.

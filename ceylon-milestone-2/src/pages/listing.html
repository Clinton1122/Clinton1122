About this tour

Kandy is the cultural heart of Sri Lanka, a city wrapped in green hills around a lake, with temples, palaces and markets that have been part of daily life for centuries. This private day tour is led by a licensed guide who grew up here.

You’ll start at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic before the crowds, walk the lake and the old royal palace, taste your way through the central market, and finish at the Bahirawakanda Buddha with the whole city below you. Lunch is at a family-run rice-and-curry place your guide knows well.

The day runs at your pace. If you’d rather spend longer at the temple or skip the market, just say so on the day.

Questions

Is the Temple of the Tooth entry included?

Yes. Shoulders and knees must be covered. Bring a scarf or sarong, or borrow one from your guide.

Can we change the order of stops?

Yes. It’s a private tour, so tell your guide on the day.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. Child seats are free. Ask for one at checkout.

What happens if it rains?

The tour runs in light rain. If the host cancels for safety, you get a full refund or a free new date.

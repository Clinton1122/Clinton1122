Notes · admin side of cancellations

How the admin screens fit together

Only requests for more than a policy gives reach admin. Cancellations inside a policy are refunded automatically, and a weekly sample is checked. Everything else is a queue the team works through, with the list and the open request side by side.

1 · A request comes in

With partnersWaiting for the partner12 or 24 hour window with a countdown, an automatic reminder at 3 hours left, a reminder button and “Decide now” for urgent cases (reason required).
To decideThree facts, then the decisionWhat the policy gives (with a warning if it changed after booking), what the partner said, and the traveller's history. Ordered by when the trip starts.
Decide%, fee, reasonRefund is a % of the partner's price; VAT and the service fee follow. Fee standard (5% of the refund, min USD 1) or waived. Three receipts: traveller, partner, Ceylon Nooks.
ClosedBoth toldEmail and WhatsApp to the partner, email to the traveller with the amount in the currency they paid.

Things to point out to Akila. Admin sees what the partner and traveller don't: the currency the traveller paid in next to USD, and Ceylon Nooks' own result on the decision, including the card fee that isn't returned (the real figure from Airwallex, as agreed). A goodwill full refund shows its real cost, USD 6.92 on Sofia's booking. When it's the partner's or our side, the refund is locked to full with no fee.

Bulk. Only requests where the partner agreed or said it was their side, the trip hasn't started, the policy version matches and nothing is flagged. The dialog shows each one and the totals before anything is sent.

2 · Custom policies and checks

Policies tab. The current and proposed versions drawn side by side with the same policy line partners and travellers see, an example with real dates and the pay later date, automatic checks (including “stricter than now”), the partner's note, and Approve, Edit tiers or Send back with a note. Existing bookings always keep the version they booked under.

Checks. 10 automatic refunds a week, picked at random. Mark right or flag. A flag never takes money back from a traveller; it goes to finance to find the cause. The example flag is a tier that ended in the wrong time zone.

Numbers used

Akila's pricing model: price, VAT 18% when the partner is registered, a 10% service fee, commission set per partner (12%, 10% and 8% in these examples), VAT handling fee 4.42% of the VAT. Card fees are examples of what Airwallex reports.

Not in this batch

The Ledger (waiting for Akila's go-ahead) and the payout run review. The admin booking list with margin columns comes with the later admin redesign.

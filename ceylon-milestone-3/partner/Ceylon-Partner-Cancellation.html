Notes · partner side of cancellations

How the partner screens fit together

There are three ways a partner deals with cancellations. These notes go with the designs in section 1, in the order a partner would meet them.

1 · A traveller asks for more than the policy gives

Refunds listRequest cardShows who's asking, why, the policy as dates, and how long the partner has to reply.
ReplyPick one of four repliesOffer another date, agree a refund (full, half or an amount), keep the policy (reason required), or say it was on their side.
SentWith our teamA receipt shows what the traveller gets and the new payout. The partner can change the reply or add a note until we decide.
OutcomeDecidedWhatsApp and email with the new payout. The request moves to Closed.

Things to point out to Akila. The receipt updates as soon as the partner picks a reply, so they see what it costs them before sending. “Offer another date” lists real dates with free spots. “It was on our side” cancels straight away with a full refund and no fee, so it uses the dark button and a warning instead of orange.

Edge cases covered. A reply without a reason when keeping the policy shows an error. If the window closes with no reply, the panel says so and the partner can still add a note. A trip in under 3 days gets a 12 hour window and a red clock.

2 · The partner can't run a trip

Bookings or TodayPick the dayDays with a weather warning show a rain mark.
One booking or the whole dayCancelReason, optional evidence (photo or link), and “Stop new bookings for this date”. The receipt shows the total going back to travellers.
DoneTravellers refunded in fullNo fee. We message each traveller with other dates and similar trips. The partner's payout for those bookings becomes 0.

3 · The partner sets the policy

Listing form, step 4Choose a policyFour presets, each with a small bar showing its shape, or Custom.
CustomUp to 4 tiers“If cancelled at least 14 days before, 100% back.” The last row updates itself. Replaces today's free-text box and separate Refund Schedule card.
PreviewWhat travellers seeThe same policy as real dates for an example trip, plus the pay later charge date. Custom goes to our team before the listing is live.

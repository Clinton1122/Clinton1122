How refunds show up in a partner's money

Partners are paid in LKR every Monday and Thursday for bookings that have ended, one transfer per currency. A refund can reach their money in three ways, and each one has its own place on screen and on the statement.

Before payoutOn the booking's own lineThe refund is agreed before the booking is paid out (Daniel, half refunded). The booking shows a “Refund, your share” amount and a small tag, and the partner receives the rest.
After payoutAs its own line in the next payoutThe partner was already paid, then a refund was agreed (Mark, goodwill). It shows under “Refunds after an earlier payout”, with the date it was first paid.
Bigger than the payoutCarried to the next payoutThe refund is more than the payout's bookings (Noah, USD 278.80). Nothing is sent, the payout says why, and the rest comes off the next payout. The partner never has to pay us back directly.

The numbers, all the same rule

The partner's share of a refund is the refund times their payout rate (82% in these examples; each partner's real rate is set in admin). Daniel's half refund is USD 60.00, so Arkam's share is USD 49.20. The 5% processing fee is paid by the traveller and never touches the partner. When a partner cancels, the refund is the whole booking, so their payout for it becomes 0.

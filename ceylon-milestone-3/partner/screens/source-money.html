How refunds show up in a partner's money

Partners are paid in LKR every Monday and Thursday for bookings that have ended, one transfer per currency. A refund can reach their money in three ways, and each one has its own place on screen and on the statement.

Before payoutOn the booking's own lineThe refund is agreed before the booking is paid out (Daniel, 50%). The booking is listed at its full amount and the refund shows under “Refunds and cancellations” in the same payout.
After payoutAs its own line in the next payoutThe partner was already paid, then a refund was agreed (Mark, goodwill). It shows under “Refunds and cancellations” in the next payout, saying when it was first paid.
Bigger than the payoutCarried to the next payoutThe refund is more than the payout's bookings (Noah, USD 300.17). Nothing is sent, the payout says why, and the rest comes off the next payout. The partner never has to pay us back directly.

The numbers follow Akila's pricing model

Per booking the partner sees their price, VAT collected for them, commission on their price, the VAT handling fee (4.42% of the VAT) and what they receive, in USD. A refund is a percentage of their price; the same percentage of the VAT and service fee goes back automatically, and 5% of the amount refunded is kept as a processing fee. The partner's share is what they would have received on the refunded part. Example: Daniel, price USD 100.00, receives USD 107.20; a 50% refund leaves USD 53.60. The service fee and the processing fee are never taken from the partner. There's no transfer fee line and no exchange fee line: the rate shown is the one used. Commission is 10% in these examples; each partner's own rate is set in admin.

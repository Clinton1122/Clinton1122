The rest of the partner dashboard, and how a partner cancels

Seven tabs, one job each. Today puts what needs the partner first, at full size. Every other tab is a place to look things up or change them. Cancelling an accepted booking exists, as Akila asked, but it's deliberately hard to reach.

Step 1 · Booking pageNo cancel buttonThe booking page only offers helpful actions. At the very bottom, in grey: “Something wrong with this booking? Get help”.
Step 2 · What's the problem?Alternatives firstMove to another date, change the guide or vehicle, message the traveller, cancel a day for weather, or ask our team. “Cancel this booking” is a quiet link under them.
Step 3 · Cancel formReason, consequences, confirmA reason and a note are required. The partner sees what they lose, types the booking reference and ticks a box. Within 48 hours of the trip they must call us instead.
Step 4 · Our teamChecked before it happensThe booking stays on until our team confirms, within 2 hours. We try to save it first. Only then is the traveller told and fully refunded.

Questions for Akila

1. Should our team check every partner cancellation (as designed), or should it happen straight away?
2. Is 48 hours the right cut-off for “call us instead”?
3. What happens after repeated cancellations? The design says our team reviews listings at 3 in 12 months. Is there a penalty fee?
4. Weather cancellations of a whole day don't count against the partner. Agreed?
5. The partner team phone and WhatsApp numbers shown are placeholders. What are the real ones?

How the tabs link

Today links to the refund reply, the message thread and the day's bookings. A booking links to its messages, its money and its refund request if there is one. Money has Payouts (already designed), Statements and Bank details. Account holds business details, VAT status, team and WhatsApp or email settings. The Ledger stays admin only, as agreed.

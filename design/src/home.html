Before you buy

Ask us anything else on WhatsApp. A person answers.

Do I still need an inverter if I buy a PowerBox?

No. The inverter is already inside. A PowerBox holds a hybrid pure sine wave inverter, a lithium battery, a charge controller and a protection system in one unit.

Can I charge it with NEPA?

Yes. Every PowerBox charges three ways: from solar panels, from a wall socket, or from the grid when it comes.

Can it power my fridge?

From the PowerBox 1500 up, a table-top fridge up to 120L. A full double-door fridge or a chest freezer needs the PowerBox 2600 or bigger.

Can it run my air conditioner?

Only the heavy-duty range. The PowerBox 4000 and 8000 run one inverter AC, best during the day with panels. The 8000 Plus runs one or two 1hp inverter ACs.

Can I buy the box now and add panels later?

Yes. Every model is sold box only or with its panels, and panels can be added any time. Nothing inside the box changes.

Do you deliver outside Akure and Lagos?

Yes, nationwide from both offices. Call or WhatsApp 0706 287 8273.

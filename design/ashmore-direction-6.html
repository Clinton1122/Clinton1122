    Home / Nervous patients

    Nervous about the dentist? Start here.

    Lots of people feel this way, and plenty haven't been in for years. This page tells you what to expect. It also lets you write down what would help, so the team knows before you sit down.

    What would make it easier?

    Tick anything that sounds right. Your note builds as you go.

    Things that would help

    Your note for the team

    Tick something and your note appears here.

    Read it out when you call (03) 5331 1959, or paste it into an email to hello@ashmoredentistry.com.au.

    Tell the team which of these matter most. They'll plan your visit around them.

    What your first visit looks like.

    Four steps, at your pace.

    1. You get in touch

      Call or email, and say you're nervous. That's all you need to do.

    2. You talk first

      Before anything happens, you sit down with your dentist and say what worries you.

    3. You decide together

      Your dentist explains the options. You choose what happens and when.

    4. You stay in control

      Use your signal whenever you need a break. Everything stops.

    Sleep dentistry

    If you'd rather not be awake for it.

    Sleep dentistry uses medication so you're deeply relaxed, or asleep, while your dentist works. It can help if a visit feels too much to manage awake, or if you need a lot of treatment in one go.

    Is it right for me?

    Your dentist checks your health and the treatment you need before recommending it.

    How do I prepare?

    You'll get clear written instructions, including when to stop eating and drinking.

    Getting home

    Bring someone who can take you home and stay with you for the rest of the day.

    What it costs

    You'll get a written quote before you book.

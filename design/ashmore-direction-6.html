    Ashmore House

    Built in the gold rush. Still looking after Ballarat.

    Ashmore House went up at the height of Ballarat's gold rush in the late 1800s: brickwork, decorative cornices and cast-iron lace on the verandah. The practice inside has been part of town for 35 years, and became Ashmore Dentistry in 2019.

    What your first visit looks like.

    Ashmore to check this matches how they work

    1. You get in touch

      Call or email, and say you're nervous. That's all you need to do.

    2. You talk first

      Before anything happens, you sit down with your dentist and say what worries you.

    3. You decide together

      Your dentist explains the options. You choose what happens and when.

    4. You stay in control

      Use your signal whenever you need a break. Everything stops.

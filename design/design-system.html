Yellow is light.

How the Lumey site looks, sounds and behaves. Every rule here comes from one fact about the business: people buy a PowerBox for the night NEPA takes light.

Day and night
Day sections (paper, haze, sun) are where people compare prices and buy. Night sections show the PowerBox working during a power cut. A page moves between the two on purpose, never at random.
Yellow means on
Yellow fills what is powered, what is chosen and the next step. It is never decoration and never body text on white. If nothing is on, nothing is yellow.
The bars are the range
The logo's stepped bars become the eight PowerBoxes, each as tall as its battery. They pick a model, measure a battery and show where a model sits. Never drawn at random heights.

Logo

The mark is the yellow stepped bars. Until the original file is in hand, the pages set the name in Big Shoulders. The mark is never redrawn by hand.

Logo file needed

Yellow mark on night. Save as assets/lumey-logo.svg

Black version needed

Black mark on sun, for the yellow sections.

  • On night and on ink, the mark is yellow. On sun, it is black. On paper and haze, it is black with yellow allowed only if the file provides it.
  • Never smaller than 32px tall in the header, 24px anywhere else.
  • Keep clear space of one bar's width on every side.
  • Never recolour individual bars, stretch the mark or set it on a photograph without a solid ground behind it.

Questions

Native disclosure, so every answer is in the page for search engines and for people without JavaScript. First answer opens by default.

Can I charge it with NEPA?

Yes. Every PowerBox charges three ways: from solar panels, from a wall socket, or from the grid when it comes.

Can it run my air conditioner?

Only the heavy-duty range, from the PowerBox 4000.

The outage scene

A section through a Nigerian bungalow at night: parlour, kitchen, two bedrooms, the overhead tank and the neighbour on a generator. It shows what a model runs. It is the signature of the site.

  • Lit and drawn in ink means the model runs it. Grey with a cross means it doesn't. The same facts always appear in words beside the drawing.
  • What each model runs comes from the product data, never from the drawing. Change the data and the drawing follows.
  • Only the neighbour's window uses flame orange, and it is the only thing on the site that moves by itself. It stops for anyone who prefers reduced motion.
  • Run-times are not drawn until they are measured.

Motion

Things move when someone does something, to show what changed. Nothing fades in on scroll.

  • Switching NEPA off cuts the scene to dark for under half a second, then the PowerBox brings the rooms back. That is the one dramatic moment on the site.
  • Choosing a model: bars and appliances change colour over 150ms. Hover: colour only, 120ms. Nothing scales or bounces.
  • The buy bar slides up in 200ms. The neighbour's window flickers. Nothing else moves on its own.
  • With reduced motion turned on, every change is instant and the flicker stops.
